Anton Xue

Postdoctoral Fellow, UT Austin

I work on diffusion language models, AI agents for mathematical research, and provable AI safety and interpretability. I'm currently at the Institute for Foundations of Machine Learning (IFML) at UT Austin, working with Sanjay Shakkottai and Adam Klivans.

I completed my Ph.D. at the University of Pennsylvania with Rajeev Alur and Eric Wong.
